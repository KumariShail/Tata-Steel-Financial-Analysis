# Tata-Steel-Financial-Analysis
Financial Performance Analysis of Tata Steel Products Downstream Limited using Excel and Power BI
Tata Steel Downstream Products Limited – Financial Performance Analysis

Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing the financial performance of Tata Steel Downstream Products Limited using Microsoft Excel and Power BI.

The analysis compares the company's financial performance across FY 2023–24 and FY 2024–25 to understand revenue trends, profitability, expenses, assets, liabilities and cash flow movements.

Objectives

- Analyse year-on-year financial performance.
- Evaluate profitability and financial ratios.
- Examine operating, investing and financing cash flows.
- Identify significant financial changes.
- Develop meaningful business insights from financial data.

Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Excel
- Microsoft Power BI
- Financial Statement Analysis
- Data Visualization
- Business Analytics

Project Components

1. Excel Financial Analysis

- Financial data organisation.
- Year-on-year comparison.
- Financial ratio analysis.
- Cash flow analysis.
- Business insights.

2. Power BI Dashboard

- Financial KPI cards.
- Revenue and expense analysis.
- Profitability comparison.
- Asset analysis.
- Interactive visualizations.

Key Findings

- Revenue from operations declined by approximately 2.50%.
- Profit after tax declined by approximately 7.58%.
- Operating cash flow increased by approximately 117.93%.
- Current ratio increased from 3.05 to 4.37.
- Exceptional expenses associated with the medical and separation scheme provisions were identified as an important factor for further profitability analysis.

Business Insights

The analysis highlights the importance of examining profitability, liquidity and cash flow together rather than relying on revenue alone.

It also identifies areas for further investigation, including working-capital movements, inventory management and operating expenses.
