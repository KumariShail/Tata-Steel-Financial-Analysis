📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing the financial performance of Tata Steel Downstream Products Limited using Microsoft Excel and Power BI.

The analysis covers two financial years (FY 2023–24 and FY 2024–25) to evaluate the company's financial position, profitability, operational performance and cash flow movements.

The objective was to transform financial statement data into meaningful visualizations and business insights that help understand the company's financial performance.

---

🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyse year-on-year financial performance.
- Evaluate revenue, expenditure and profitability trends.
- Calculate and interpret important financial ratios.
- Examine operating, investing and financing cash flows.
- Identify significant changes in assets and liabilities.
- Develop meaningful business insights from financial data.

🔍 Key Financial Findings

The analysis identified the following changes between FY 2023–24 and FY 2024–25:

Financial Indicator| Change
Revenue from Operations| -2.50%
Profit After Tax| -7.58%
Operating Cash Flow| +117.93%
Current Ratio| 3.05 → 4.37

Important Observations

- Revenue from operations declined slightly during the financial year.
- Profit after tax declined at a higher rate than revenue.
- Operating cash flow increased significantly.
- The current ratio improved, indicating a stronger current liquidity position.
- Exceptional expenses associated with medical and separation scheme provisions were identified for further profitability analysis.

---

💡 Business Insights

The financial analysis highlights several important areas for business investigation:

1. Revenue Performance: Investigating the reasons behind the decline in revenue from operations.

2. Profitability: Understanding why profitability declined more significantly than revenue.

3. Cash Flow Management: Examining the factors contributing to the increase in operating cash flow.

4. Inventory Management: Understanding the impact of inventory movements on working capital.

5. Liquidity Position: Evaluating the improvement in the company's current ratio.

6. Financial Efficiency: Examining the relationship between operating expenses, profitability and financial performance.
